# F1: HMS Queen Mary 1913 服役原状识别特征

## Findings

### [1] Queen Mary 与 Lion/Princess Royal 同代但常被归为独立设计，差异集中在副炮与装甲布置、军官舱位置
- quote: "HMS Queen mary on her side was ordered at the same time as the four King George V class battleships under the 1910–11 Naval Programme... She differed from the Lion class in her secondary armament and armour layout, as well as the location of the officers' quarters."
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: medium

### [2] 灰盒级识别差异：副炮位于单层甲板、中烟囱比 Lion 级更圆、有近姊妹舰没有的 sternwalk；原为 pole mast 后改 tripod
- quote: "Visually she was hard to distinguish from the Lion class but with the secondary armament on a single deck, the centre funnel was rounder than in the Lion class and she had a stern walkway that was lacking in her near sisters. Originally fitted with a pole mast this was later altered to a tripod mast."
- url: http://www.worldwar1.co.uk/battlecruiser/hms-queen-mary.html
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown (site copyright 1998–2026)
- confidence: medium

### [3] 主炮布局为 4 座联装轴线炮塔（A/B/Q/Y），前两座超射，后两座间有明显间隔；未采用 Orion 式第五座后部超射炮塔
- quote: "On the three Lions, they were installed in four axial turrets, two superfiring forward, two aft with an important gap between \"Q\" and \"Y\"... suggested fifth turret superfiring over the rear turret... However this was not approved"
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: high

### [4] 建成副炮为 16 门 BL 4-inch Mk VII，单装、左右各 8 门、全部位于 casemates
- quote: "sixteen BL 4-inch Mark VII guns, eigh per side, all in single mounts, casemated"
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: high

### [5] Queen Mary 首次将军官舱恢复到舰艉，并被记为皇家海军首舰装备 sternwalk
- quote: "after complaints from the officers, HMS Queen Mary was the first to restore them in the stern, the old way. She however was the first also to mount a sternwalk."
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: medium

### [6] 1913 服役原状不应出现 AA 炮：3-inch 20 cwt 与 QF 6-pounder Hotchkiss 记载为 1914 年 10 月加装
- quote: "Anti-aircraft guns were fitted in October 1914, a single QF 6-pounder Hotchkiss gun and a QF 3-inch 20 cwt."
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: high

### [7] 建造期烟囱/桅杆修正：非 Lion 原案；前烟囱后移、前后桅对调、foremast 改 pole、三烟囱高度拉齐以减烟干扰
- quote: "In effect, the fore funnel was moved aft, and the original fore and mainmasts swapped position. The foremast was converted into a pole mast... All the funnels were raised to the same height also to better control smoke interference."
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: medium

### [8] 常见误识别：HMS Tiger（1914）副炮为 12 门 6-inch casemates，而 Queen Mary 为 16 门 4-inch；Tiger 炮塔/上层建筑/烟囱高度与位置全部重排，不能当作 1913 Queen Mary 外观
- quote: "Armaments wise, turrets placement and superstructure were completely revised, as well as the position and height of funnels... twelve BL 6-inch Mk VII guns total"
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/hms-tiger.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2019-12-08
- confidence: high

### [9] 另一类常见误识别：历史文章常只把 Lion 与 Princess Royal 列为 Lion 级；Queen Mary 常被单独列出——建模时勿默认“三舰同模”
- quote: "Ships in class: HMS Lion — HMS Princess Royal"
- url: http://www.historyofwar.org/articles/weapons_lion_class_battlecruisers.html
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2007-11-15
- confidence: medium

### [10] MaritimeQuest 建成数据：Palmer's Jarrow 铺设龙骨 1911-03-06、下水 1912-03-20、服役 1913-04-09；建成武备 8×13.5"/45 Mk V 四联装 + 16×4"/50 单装 casemates + 2×21" 舷侧水下鱼雷管
- quote: "Keel Laid: March 6, 1911 / Launched: March 20, 1912 / Commissioned: April 9, 1913... Armament as built: 8 13.5\"/45 (343mm) Mk V (H) 4 twin turrets; 16 4\"/50 (102mm) BL Mk VII Single mounts casemates; 2 21\" (533mm) torpedo tubes broadside submerged"
- url: https://www.maritimequest.com/warship_directory/great_britain/battleships/queen_mary/hms_queen_mary_data.htm
- source_type: community

### [11] 尺度数据存在源间冲突，灰盒标定需选定单一权威：MaritimeQuest 给 700 ft / 88'9" / 27,300–30,710 t；Naval Encyclopedia 给 700 ft 0.6 in (213.4 m) / 89 ft 0.5 in / 26,770–31,650 long tons；worldwar1.co.uk 给水线 698 ft / 全长 703 ft 6 in / 89 ft
- quote: "Length: 700' Beam: 88'9\" Displacement: 27,300 std. / 30,710 full"

### [12] 战时外观改动（不可用于 1913 原状）：AA 增强、桅杆改回 tripod、射程延长、鱼雷防护网拆除——1913 灰盒应保留鱼雷网+pole mast+无 AA
- quote: "During the war their AA artillery was improved, their mast became tripod again and firing range extended, while their torpedo nets were removed altogether."
- url: https://www.naval-encyclopedia.com/ww1/uk/lion-class-battlecruisers.php
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2016-05-22
- confidence: medium

### [13] 同屏识别参考：1st BCS Jutland 序列中 Queen Mary 与 Lion、Princess Royal、Tiger 同队，标注 26,770 t、8×13.5-inch、28 kn——50–200 m 观察时需靠副炮层数/烟囱形状/sternwalk 区分而非主炮
- quote: "HMS Lion 1912-26,270 tons-8X13.5 inch guns-28 kts... HMS Princess Royal 1912-26,270 tons-8X13.5 inch guns-28 kts... HMS Queen Mary 1912-26,770 tons-8X13.5 inch guns-28 kts... HMS Tiger 1914-28,430 tons-8X13.5 inch guns-28 kts"
